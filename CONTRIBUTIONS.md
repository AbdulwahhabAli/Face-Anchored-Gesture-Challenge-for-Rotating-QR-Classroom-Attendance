## What the team built (this repository)

- The **face-anchored ROI cascade** (`src/gesture_attendance/vision/roi.py`, `pipelines.py`): the ROI
  geometry, crop/up-scale handling, mapping detections back to full-image coordinates, the no-face fallback, and
  the controlled baseline that uses the same detector weights.
- The **attendance system** (`src/gesture_attendance/app/`): rotating-QR tokens, phone-bound tickets, the random
  gesture challenge, enrollment with instructor confirmation, flags, and Excel reports.
- The **evaluation protocol and code**: the HaGRID subset fetcher that uses the official subject-disjoint split
  (`scripts/fetch_hagrid.py`), the deterministic four-condition test set including the background-hand attack
  (`scripts/make_conditions.py`), paired statistics (bootstrap CI, exact McNemar), end-to-end FRR/FAR, latency
  benchmark, analysis and figures (`scripts/predict.py`, `evaluate.py`, `analyze.py`, `report_figures.py`).
- **Fine-tuning** YOLOv8n on 1,800 HaGRID images (`scripts/train_yolo.py`). The weights are ours; the
  architecture and COCO initialisation come from Ultralytics.
